Paper Alarm is an offline alarm and birthday reminder app for Android 13 and later, with a paper-inspired interface and Android system fonts.

• One-time and weekly alarms, labels, five tones and adjustable snooze duration.
• Solar and Chinese lunar birthdays with yearly ringing reminders, optional birth year, cards and lists.
• Import birthdays from Android contacts or write them to an existing or new contact. Lunar birthdays use a custom “农历生日” event. Writing replaces the existing birthday in the same calendar.
• Ringing notifications and lock-screen reminders, subject to Android permissions and device background restrictions.

If a lunar leap month is absent, the regular month is used; missing days use the end of the month.

No Internet permission, advertising, analytics or account is required. Alarm data stays in app-private storage and is removed on uninstall. Contacts permissions are optional for contact features. Your system contact account may sync birthdays written to contacts independently of this app.

Notifications, exact alarms, full-screen display, overlay access and battery/background settings support ringing. Test an alarm on your device after configuring these settings.
